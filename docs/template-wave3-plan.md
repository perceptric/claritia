# Templates, wave 3: simple one-page forms, plain multi-page forms, and long forms split into pages

Status: plan. 8 November 2026. Builds on `docs/form-templates.md` (the gallery, its menus and format) and
`docs/template-pictures-plan.md` (the photos).

**The goal:** every kind of form has a few simple versions for simple needs, the bigger jobs get more multi-page forms
with no photos, and the long one-page forms that ask too much on one screen become pages.

## Part 2. Simple one-page forms: 265 new

**3 new simple forms in every category, 4 in the ten busiest** (Lead generation, Quote, Consultation, Appointment,
Booking, Application, Event registration, Feedback, Survey, Request).

**The rules for a simple form:**
- One page, 3 to 6 questions, one job. Email is one of them unless the form is anonymous.
- No welcome screen and no photo: it opens on the questions.
- No logic, calculations or payments. A choice or two is fine.
- Plain words, one short line under the title, a send button that says what happens ("Send", "Request a call",
  "Join the list").
- Each gets its category, 1 to 3 industries and a team, so it shows in every menu like the rest.
- Its name says how it differs from the full version: "Quick quote", "Short feedback", "Two-minute intake".

## Part 4. Long one-page forms become pages: 60

parts** get split into 2 or 3 pages: about you, the job, the details. Today that's these 60:

- **Leads and sales (9):** media.podcast-guest, security.system-quote, agriculture.equipment-demo,
  food.wholesale-inquiry, manufacturing.rfq, saas.partner-program, creative.photography-inquiry, pets.puppy-waitlist,
  media.advertiser-brief
- **Apply and join (4):** faith.membership, sports.coach-application, real_estate.coworking-membership,
  education.field-trip
- **Feedback (2):** hospitality.tour-review, education.graduate-story
- **Operations (12):** it.equipment-request, it.software-request, it.lost-device, retail.return-authorization,

  construction.pest-inspection, manufacturing.machine-downtime, general.data-access-request, saas.account-closure

**Left as one page on purpose:** checklists and logs (staff tick down one list), consents, waivers and agreements
(read and signed in one place), and quizzes (they already ask one question at a time).

**How a split works:** the same questions in the same order, with the same keys, so routing, workflows and old
results keep working. Show-if rules stay; a rule that hides everything on a page becomes a page skip. Forms that open
on a welcome photo keep it.

## Part 5. The gallery, the website and the checks

- **Gallery:** 740 templates. "Simple" becomes a value in the Length menu (under 1 minute, 6 questions or
  fewer), so simple forms can be filtered without burying the full ones. Most used still sorts first.
- **App size:** Forms › Templates loads the whole catalog in the browser today (`TemplatesPage.tsx` imports
  `catalog.ts`, 588 KB of source). It moves behind a lazy import, like the Studio's gallery already does, so the
  page's first load doesn't grow.
- **Website:** 305 more template pages, rebuilt from the export as today.
- **Checks** (`tests/formGallery.test.ts` and new rules):
  - Every template converts to a Studio page and runs: the existing tests cover all of them.
  - Simple forms: one page, 3 to 6 questions, no welcome screen, no logic or payment.
  - New multi-page forms: 3 to 6 pages, no photo, every page has a title, every page skip points forward.
  - Split forms keep exactly the same question keys and order as before (a snapshot taken before the split).
  - No two templates share a name or slug; every template has a category, an industry and a team.
  - `scripts/templates-check.ts` and the website screenshots for one form of each kind.
