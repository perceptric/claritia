# Form templates: the plan past Fillout

Today: 150 templates, 24 industries, 10 use cases and 18 styles, in Forms › Templates and on tryclaritia.com/templates
(`docs/form-templates.md`). Fillout sorts about 550 template tags into six menus: Layout, Category (36), Industry,
Quizzes, Surveys and Role, with a "Make a form with AI" box at the end. This plan matches every menu and then adds what
only Claritia can offer, because a Claritia form books the meeting, routes the lead and starts the follow-up.

## Status (2026-11-03)

Built: steps 1 to 5 and most of 6 (`docs/form-templates.md`). 424 templates (the first 150 and wave 1's 274), the menus
with 85 categories, 38 industries and 16 teams, wired templates, starter kits for 36 industries, the seasonal row,
the live demos with calculations, payments and a sample booking step, and Describe your form.
The website has 276 list pages and 424 template pages. Two changes from the plan: "Routes to a teammate" is left out
(a template can't know a workspace's teams), and a booking template books the workspace's meeting only when it has
exactly one. Still to do: wave 2 (to about 600), languages, real "Most used" counts, and carrying a brand picked on the
website into the new workspace.

## Part 1. The menus

Nine menus on the gallery, in this order. Menus 1 to 4 are tagged by hand on each template. Menus 5 to 9 are worked
out from the form itself, so a tag can never be wrong.

| # | Menu | Values | How it's set |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Category | About 70, in 10 families (Part 2) | 1 to 3 per template, by hand |
| 2 | Industry | About 40, with sub-industries (Part 3) | 1 or more per template, by hand |
| 3 | Team | 16 (Part 4) | 1 to 3 per template, by hand |
| 4 | Quizzes and surveys | Their own families inside Category, shown as a menu too | By hand |
| 5 | What it does next | Books a meeting, Routes to a teammate, Takes payment, Shows a price, Shows a score, Sends a follow-up, Sends to your CRM, Anonymous | From the template's kit (Part 5) |
| 6 | Features | Logic, Calculator, Payment, Booking, Score, File upload, Signature, Grid, Ranking, Address, Hidden fields | From the fields and rules |
| 7 | Layout | One page, Steps, One question at a time, Cover, Picture beside | From `layout` and the step count |
| 8 | Length | Under 1 minute, 1 to 3 minutes, Over 3 minutes | From the questions, with a time per kind |
| 9 | Style | The 18 styles | Any template in any style |

Rules for the menus:

- A value shows only when 4 or more templates have it. Below that it stays hidden, so no menu leads to a near-empty page.
- Every count beside a value is the real count, worked out when the gallery is built.
- Picking more than one value inside a menu widens the results. Picking in two menus narrows them.
- Sort: Most used, Newest, A to Z. "Most used" uses real counts of Use template clicks and shows only once a template
  has 25 or more.

## Part 5. Past Fillout: what only Claritia can offer

### 5.1 Templates come wired

A template is a form plus what happens after the send. Use template sets up the whole flow in one click:

- **On Submission:** the thank-you or the meeting, and the routes by answer (a quote over $10,000 goes to the senior
  rep). A booking template makes its meeting type if the workspace has none.
- **Workflow:** the follow-up emails, the reminder before the meeting, the Slack ping. Made as a draft workflow in a
  folder named after the template, off until the owner turns it on.
- **Sends to:** the CRM fields the answers map to, used when HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Attio or Close is
  connected.

The gallery card and the template page show the flow as a short line of steps: Form, then Meeting, then Follow-up.
This feeds menu 5.

### 5.2 Starter kits

A kit is 3 to 6 wired templates for one kind of business, made in one click: a law firm gets Contact, Case intake,
Consultation booking and Client feedback. One kit for each of the 40 industries. Kits get their own row at the top of
each industry page.

### 5.3 See it in your brand

Removed from the website on 3 November 2026 (the yoursite.com box and `/api/templates/brand`). In the app,
cards and previews still switch to the workspace's own brand kits.

- In the app, every card and preview can switch to the workspace's own brand kit, with its logo, colors and fonts.
  The 18 styles stay as the other choice.
- On the website, a visitor types their site address and the gallery redraws in their colors, fonts and logo. The look
  is read from the page source with no AI call. Use template carries it into sign-up, so the first form is already in
  their brand. Limits: 5 a visitor a day, 2,000 a day in total.

### 5.4 Try it for real

Every template page runs the real form: logic, a calculator's price, a quiz's score and result, and the meeting step
with sample open times. Nothing is saved or sent. The end screen says what would happen next ("Your team gets this lead
in HubSpot").

### 5.5 Describe your form

The box at the end of every search and every menu page: "Describe the form you need". In the app it opens a new form
in Form Studio with the words as the first message to Tia. On the website the words are kept through sign-up and the
build starts on the first screen.

Before building, the words are matched to the catalog (Claude Haiku, through the step cache). When a template fits,
it's offered first: "Start from Roofing estimate". The same match powers search when the words don't hit a name or tag,
so "deposit for cake orders" finds Bakery order.

### 5.6 Collections

Rows on the gallery's first screen, each with a real rule behind it:

- Most used this month, once counts are high enough.
- New this month.
- Seasonal: Back to school (July to September), Tax season (January to April), Holiday orders (October to December),
  Summer camp (February to May), Year-end reviews (November to December).
- Made for booking, made for payment and made for scoring, from menu 5.

### 5.7 Languages

Every template in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Dutch, through Form Studio's translation step. The
template page and Use template take the language. Menu 10 when it ships.

## Part 7. The app

`TemplatesPage.tsx` gets the menus as a left rail, each menu folding open, with the families as the first level of
Category. Picked values show as tags above the grid, each with a remove. On small screens the rail opens in a Sheet.
Every menu uses the app's `Select` and pill controls. The first screen shows kits and collections; any search or pick
switches to the grid.

## Part 8. Data

Added to `FormTemplate` (`src/lib/forms/gallery/types.ts`):

| Field | Type | Set |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `categories` | 1 to 3 category ids | By hand |
| `industries` | 1 or more industry or sub-industry ids (replaces `industry`) | By hand |
| `teams` | 1 to 3 team ids | By hand |
| `kit` | On Submission, routes, workflow draft and CRM field map | By hand, checked |
| `seasons` | Months it's featured | By hand, optional |
| `added` | Date it joined the catalog | By hand |
| `features`, `layout`, `length`, `does` | Menus 5 to 8 | Worked out, never stored |

Tests (`tests/formGallery.test.ts`):

- Every template passes `checkForm`, and every kit passes the On Submission and workflow checks.
- Every category, industry and team id exists, and every shown value has 4 or more templates.
- A template's worked-out features match its fields (a "Payment" tag means a payment block is there).
- No two templates in one category have the same questions in the same order.
- Every website page in the sitemap exists, and every page links back to its family.

## Part 9. Writing 450 more templates

- The grid of Part 2 against Part 3 shows each gap. Waves fill the largest gaps first.
- Claude Opus writes each draft from a short brief: category, industry, who fills it in, what happens next. Each draft
  runs through `checkForm` and the tests above, then a person reads it before it joins.
- Questions follow the same rules as the first 150: real wording a business would use, the email first when it's a lead
  form, no more than 12 questions on a step, and show-if logic only where it saves the visitor a question.

## Part 11. Running cost

Claude Opus 5.5 at $4 in and $20 out per million tokens, Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10 in and $0.50 out (check: from this
repo's price table, not confirmed on Anthropic's page from here).

Assumptions: each team builds 3 more forms a month from the Describe box (about $0.90 each, the same as a Tia build).
Catalog matches run on 20,000 searches a month at Beta and 200,000 at Growth (about 2,000 tokens in and 100 out each,
repeats free from the step cache).
